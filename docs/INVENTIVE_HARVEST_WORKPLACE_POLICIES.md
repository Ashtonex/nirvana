## 1. INTRODUCTION, PURPOSE & SCOPE

### 1.1. Purpose
Inventive Harvest is committed to building an exemplary, profitable, and highly disciplined commercial retail business. The purpose of this Workplace Policy Manual is to define operational expectations, standardize processes across all trading branches, safeguard Company capital and assets, and cultivate a safe, productive, and fair working environment.

### 1.2. Scope
This Policy Manual applies uniformly to all employees of Inventive Harvest, regardless of rank, employment status (permanent, fixed-term, probationary, or casual), or geographic workstation—including personnel operating at **Dub Dub**, **Kipasa**, **Trade Center**, central warehousing, or executive administration.

### 1.3. Binding Nature
Compliance with this manual is a mandatory condition of employment. Ignorance of any policy contained herein shall not constitute a valid defense in any disciplinary inquiry. This manual is read in conjunction with the individual **Contract of Employment**.

## 2. CODE OF CONDUCT, ETHICS & BUSINESS INTEGRITY

### 2.1. Professionalism & Customer Excellence
- Every customer entering an Inventive Harvest shop must be greeted respectfully, warmly, and attended to promptly.
- Staff must maintain a neat, clean, and professional appearance. Where company-branded uniforms or aprons are provided, they must be worn cleanly and maintained in good repair during all working hours.
- Customer disputes or dissatisfaction must be handled calmly. Staff must never argue, shout, or display rudeness to a customer. Unresolved customer issues must be escalated immediately to the Branch Supervisor.

### 2.2. Honesty & Integrity
- Honesty is the foundational cornerstone of Inventive Harvest. Any form of deceit, manipulation of records, concealment of errors, or financial irregularities will be met with immediate disciplinary and legal action.
- Staff must immediately report any observed wrongdoing, theft, unrecorded sales, or procedural violations by colleagues or third parties to management.

### 2.3. Anti-Bribery, Gifts & Secret Commissions
- Employees are strictly prohibited from soliciting, demanding, or accepting any bribes, kickbacks, personal discounts, gifts, or secret commissions from suppliers, contractors, landlords, or customers.
- Any unsolicited gift or promotional token received from a supplier or commercial partner must be declared in writing to the Managing Director.

### 2.4. Conflict of Interest & Private Trading
- Employees shall not engage in private retail trade or sell personal merchandise of any kind on Company premises.
- Staff may not divert Inventive Harvest customers to competing suppliers or private side-businesses.
- Staff having personal or business relationships with suppliers or contractors must disclose such interests to executive management.

### 2.5. Equal Opportunity, Anti-Harassment & Anti-Bullying
- Inventive Harvest provides equal opportunity in employment and prohibits discrimination based on gender, race, tribe, religion, nationality, disability, marital status, or political affiliation.
- **Zero Tolerance for Sexual Harassment:** Any unwelcome sexual advances, requests for sexual favors, inappropriate physical contact, or sexually suggestive comments constitute gross misconduct resulting in immediate dismissal.
- Workplace bullying, intimidation, abusive language, or threatening behaviour is strictly prohibited.

## 3. SHOP OPERATIONS & POINT-OF-SALE (POS) STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURES

### 3.1. Shift Opening Procedures
1. **Punctual Arrival:** Staff rostered for the morning shift must arrive at the shop at least **15 minutes before the official opening time**.
2. **Physical Security Inspection:** Inspect external locks, grilles, windows, and display counters for signs of tampering, vandalism, or attempted forced entry before entering.
3. **Joint Float Count:** The opening cashier and the supervisor/handover colleague must open the cash safe/till and conduct a physical cash count of the opening float. The exact count must be recorded and signed in the **Daily Cash Register Control Sheet**.
4. **System Verification:** Power on the POS terminal, verify network connectivity, ensure receipt printers have adequate paper rolls, and check barcode scanners.
5. **Shop Presentation:** Dust display shelves, ensure all products have clear price labels, sweep the sales floor, and ensure lighting is fully operational.

### 3.2. Mandatory POS Sales Processing
1. **The Golden Rule: NO UNRECORDED SALES.**  
   *Every single item that leaves the shop must be entered into the POS system before being handed to the customer.*
2. **Barcode Scanning & Item Entry:** Every item sold must be scanned using the barcode scanner or selected from the accurate POS product directory. Staff must verify that the item description and price on the screen match the physical merchandise.
3. **Tendering Payment:** Enter the exact amount tendered by the customer. Verify banknote authenticity (check for counterfeit watermarks/threads) before placing cash into the till. Hand exact change to the customer.
4. **Mandatory Receipt Issuance:** Cashiers must generate and hand the official printed POS sales receipt to the customer for every transaction. It is an offense to print a receipt and discard it, or to ask a customer if they "need" a receipt.
5. **Card & Mobile Money Transactions:** For payments made via Mobile Money (EcoCash, Airtel Money, etc.) or Card POS terminals, the cashier must confirm actual receipt of funds (by verifying the reference transaction code on the official company receiving device/terminal) *before* finalizing the sale on the POS.

### 3.3. Offline / Manual Backup Protocol
- If power failure or system downtime occurs and the POS terminal is inoperative:
  - The cashier must immediately notify the Branch Supervisor.
  - Transactions must be recorded line-by-line in the pre-printed, serialized **Manual Sales Register**.
  - Information recorded must include: Date, Time, Customer Name (if known), Item Description, Quantity, Unit Price, Total Amount, and Payment Method.
  - As soon as the POS system or power is restored, all manual transactions must be entered into the POS system to update stock levels and reconcile sales.

### 3.4. Price Overrides, Discounts & Refunds
- **No Unauthorized Discounts:** Staff are strictly prohibited from altering prices or giving unauthorized discounts to friends, family, or themselves.
- **System Price Overrides:** Any price override, promotional bundle, or clearance reduction requires prior written or electronic authorization from the Managing Director or Store Supervisor.
- **Refunds & Exchanges:** No cash refund or goods exchange may be executed without:
  - An original, valid purchase receipt dated within the permitted return window;
  - Inspection of the returned goods to verify original, undamaged condition;
  - Formal signature and approval by the Branch Supervisor on the **Customer Return & Refund Voucher**.

### 3.5. Shift Closing & End-of-Day Protocol
1. **Store Lockdown:** At the official closing time, the shop doors must be secured, and only remaining inside customers may be finalized.
2. **POS End-of-Day (Z-Report):** Run the official POS End-of-Day / Z-Report summarizing total cash sales, card sales, mobile money collections, and gross turnover.
3. **Physical Cash Balancing:** Conduct a physical count of all banknotes and coins in the drawer. Compare total cash on hand against:  
   $$\text{Expected Cash} = \text{Opening Float} + \text{Cash Sales} - \text{Authorized Outflows}$$
4. **Discrepancy Declaration:** Any variance (shortage or surplus) must be documented in the closing register and reported immediately.
5. **Physical Security Lockdown:** Lock the till drawer, transfer surplus cash into the safe or secure drop-box as per protocol, turn off unnecessary electrical appliances, secure display cases, set security grilles, lock main deadbolts, and engage the alarm system.

## 4. STRICT CASH MANAGEMENT, FLOAT & OUTFLOW CONTROLS

### 4.1. Strict Separation of Financial Buckets
To prevent cash drift, unauthorized spending, and unaccountable leakage, Inventive Harvest maintains five (5) distinct, non-commingled financial categories:

| Financial Bucket | Description & Authorized Purpose | Control Mechanism |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Shop Till (Sales)** | Daily cash collected from retail customers. Used exclusively for customer change and daily banking. | Must match daily POS Z-Report exactly. No direct payments without voucher. |
| **2. Operations Fund** | Approved operational cash allocated for procurement, utilities, transport, and inventory runs. | Requires formal payment voucher with category, justification, and receipts. |
| **3. Rent Reserve** | Ring-fenced monthly funds allocated strictly to meet store rental obligations. | Must remain untouched in designated account/safe; cannot be spent on stock or petty expenses. |
| **4. Savings / Black Box** | Protected contingency reserve designed to buffer unexpected business shocks or owner capital. | Access strictly restricted to Managing Director/Owner authorization. |
| **5. Petty Cash** | Small, fixed float maintained for minor emergency store supplies (cleaning materials, urgent paper). | Maintained under the Imprest system. Every coin disbursed must have a receipt. |

### 4.2. Petty Cash & Cash Outflow Rules
- **No Cash Disbursements from the Sales Till:** Staff must not pay casual bills, transport, meals, or miscellaneous expenses directly out of daily sales takings.
- **Payment Voucher Requirement:** Every payout from Petty Cash or Operations funds must be documented on a numbered **Cash Outflow Control Invoice / Petty Cash Voucher**.
- **Mandatory Information:** The voucher must record:
  - Date and time;
  - Exact amount in figures and words;
  - Clear expense category (e.g., Shop Maintenance, Packaging, Cleaning Supplies, Approved Transport);
  - Specific payee name and signature;
  - Authorizing supervisor signature;
  - **Original VAT receipt, cash receipt, or tax invoice stapled to the voucher.**
- **Thresholds for Approval:**
  - Payouts up to \$20 (or Kwacha equivalent): Branch Supervisor approval.
  - Payouts between \$21 and \$100: Managing Director written approval.
  - Payouts exceeding \$100: Managing Director approval with executive documentation prior to disbursement.

### 4.3. Absolute Prohibition of Personal Borrowing & "IOUs"
- Under no circumstances may any employee (including supervisors and cashiers) borrow money from the till, cash drawer, petty cash, or safe for personal use.
- Placing handwritten "IOUs", "chits", or personal notes in the cash drawer in place of currency is strictly prohibited and treated as **criminal theft**.
- Mixing personal cash, personal handbags, or foreign currency inside the till drawer is strictly forbidden.

### 4.4. Cash Shortages & Cash Surpluses
- **Shortages:** Every cashier is personally accountable for the cash in their till during their shift.
  - Minor counting discrepancies must be investigated immediately.
  - Unresolved shortages resulting from failure to follow cash control SOPs or negligence must be made good by the responsible cashier, subject to lawful payroll deduction procedures.
- **Surpluses / Overages:** Any cash in excess of the POS report belongs entirely to the Company.
  - Cashiers are strictly forbidden from pocketing surpluses or keeping "secret float" reserves.
  - All surpluses must be credited to Company income in the daily closing report.

### 4.5. Banking & Cash Transit Procedures
- Banking must be conducted at designated intervals or when cash in the safe exceeds the established insurance/security threshold.
- Cash for deposit must be counted, double-verified by two staff members, placed in a sealed tamper-evident deposit bag, and accompanied by the signed deposit slip.
- Official bank deposit slips or mobile money merchant settlement statements must be returned, stamped, and archived in the financial audit folder on the same day.

## 5. INVENTORY, STOCK RECEIVING & INTER-BRANCH TRANSFERS

### 5.1. Receiving Inward Stock
1. **Verification Against Documentation:** When merchandise arrives from suppliers or central warehousing, staff must thoroughly count and inspect all cartons, items, and packaging against the official Delivery Note / Supplier Invoice.
2. **Quality & Expiry Check:** Staff must reject or flag broken, leaking, defective, or expired goods immediately.
3. **Goods Received Note (GRN):** Once verified, staff must sign the delivery slip and generate an official GRN in the inventory system.
4. **Immediate System Inwarding:** Stock must be entered into the POS system before being placed on the retail sales floor. Selling items before booking them into inventory creates negative stock and is a severe disciplinary offense.

### 5.2. Inter-Branch Stock Transfers (Dub Dub, Kipasa, Trade Center)
- Inventive Harvest strictly controls movements between its trading locations.
- **Stock Transfer Voucher (STV):** No merchandise may leave any branch for another branch without a signed, pre-numbered **Stock Transfer Voucher / Stock Issue Voucher**.
- **Dispatching Branch:** Must list exact item codes, descriptions, quantities, and condition; the issuing officer must sign.
- **Transit:** The goods must be transported directly to the destination branch without diversion.
- **Receiving Branch:** The receiving cashier/supervisor must physically count and inspect the items, sign the receiving copy, and immediately update the destination branch’s POS stock balance.
- Any variance or breakage occurring in transit must be reported to the Managing Director within two (2) hours of arrival.

### 5.3. Stocktaking & Inventory Audits
- **Routine Cycle Counts:** Each branch shall conduct weekly cycle counts of high-value and fast-moving items.
- **Monthly Wall-to-Wall Stocktake:** Full physical stock counts shall take place at the end of each calendar month across all branches.
- **Surprise Audits:** Management and internal audit teams reserve the right to perform unannounced spot stock audits at any branch at any time.
- All employees assigned to a branch are jointly responsible for inventory integrity. Unexplained stock shrinkage or missing inventory shall be investigated and may result in disciplinary action against responsible shift personnel.

### 5.4. Damaged & Expired Goods Protocol
- Items damaged in-store or identified as unsaleable must be removed immediately from public shelves to avoid customer dissatisfaction.
- The damaged item must be logged in the **Damaged Goods Register** detailing item code, description, quantity, reason for damage, and employee responsible (if caused by mishandling).
- Damaged goods may only be written off or disposed of with the written signature of the Managing Director. Staff may not take, sell at unauthorized salvage prices, or consume damaged items.

## 6. WORKING HOURS, ROSTERING, PUNCTUALITY & TIMEKEEPING

### 6.1. Work Rosters
- Work rosters for Dub Dub, Kipasa, Trade Center, and other branches shall be compiled by Branch Supervisors and approved by Management.
- Weekly rosters will be published at least **three (3) days prior** to the start of the roster week.
- Employees are required to check their schedules and report to duty on assigned shifts.

### 6.2. Shift Swapping
- Employees are not permitted to informally swap shifts without prior written permission from their Branch Supervisor.
- Shift swap requests must be submitted at least **twenty-four (24) hours in advance** and must not result in unapproved overtime or compromised staffing levels.

### 6.3. Punctuality & Time Attendance
- Employees must be on the shop floor, in uniform, and prepared to commence work at the designated shift start time.
- Arriving late disrupts handover routines and leaves customer counters unattended.
- Late arrivals exceeding ten (10) minutes without valid emergency notice shall be documented on the employee’s record. Repeated late arrivals (three instances in a month) shall attract formal disciplinary action.

### 6.4. Meal & Rest Breaks
- Meal intervals must be coordinated so that the sales counter and till are never left unmanned.
- Employees may not take lunch breaks during peak trading hours unless authorized by the supervisor.
- Eating, drinking open beverages, or snacking behind the cash desk or within sight of customers is strictly prohibited. Meals must be consumed in the designated staff break area.

## 7. LEAVE POLICIES & ABSENCE MANAGEMENT

### 7.1. Annual Leave
- Employees accumulate paid annual leave as stipulated in their Employment Contract and applicable labor law.
- Leave requests must be submitted via the formal **Leave Application Form** at least **fourteen (14) days in advance**.
- Leave approvals depend on staffing levels and business exigencies. Management reserves the right to postpone or decline leave requests during peak holiday trading seasons (e.g., Easter, Christmas, month-end stock audits).

### 7.2. Sick Leave
- If an employee is unable to report to duty due to illness, they must notify their Branch Supervisor by telephone or WhatsApp message **at least two (2) hours before their shift starts**.
- For absence of more than one (1) day, or for absence occurring immediately preceding or following a rest day or public holiday, an official medical certificate signed by a licensed medical practitioner is mandatory upon return.
- Providing fraudulent or forged medical notes is an act of gross misconduct resulting in summary dismissal and criminal referral.

### 7.3. Compassionate / Bereavement Leave
- Paid compassionate leave shall be granted in accordance with Company policy upon the death of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, biological sibling).
- The employee must provide official documentation (death certificate, burial order, or obituary notice) to validate the leave.

### 7.4. Unapproved Absence & Desertion
- Absence from work without prior permission or valid medical emergency is classified as Unapproved Absence.
- Salary shall be deducted on a *no-work, no-pay* basis for each day of unapproved absence, in addition to disciplinary action.
- Any employee absent from work for **three (3) consecutive working days** without contacting management shall be deemed to have deserted their employment, and the Company shall initiate termination procedures.

## 8. WORKPLACE SECURITY, LOSS PREVENTION & ASSET PROTECTION

### 8.1. Shop Security & Key Control
- Branch keys, padlock keys, and security safe combinations are entrusted to designated key-holders only.
- Key-holders must never duplicate keys, lend keys to colleagues, or leave keys unattended on counters.
- Loss of shop keys must be reported immediately so that locks can be replaced at once.

### 8.2. Surveillance & CCTV Awareness
- To protect staff, customers, and company assets, Inventive Harvest premises and till counters may be monitored by Closed-Circuit Television (CCTV) cameras.
- Tampering with, obstructing, disconnecting, or altering camera angles is strictly prohibited and constitutes gross misconduct.

### 8.3. Physical Search Policy
- In the interest of inventory integrity and crime prevention, Inventive Harvest reserves the right to conduct reasonable physical searches of all employees, their personal lockers, bags, coats, and parcels upon entering or leaving Company premises.
- Searches shall be conducted respectfully by an authorized person of the same gender whenever feasible.
- Refusal to submit to a reasonable search upon entering or leaving the premises constitutes insubordination.

### 8.4. Counterfeit Banknote Detection
- Cashiers must examine all high-denomination banknotes using UV counterfeit detection lamps, watermark checks, and security thread verification before completing transactions.
- Accepting an obvious counterfeit banknote due to gross failure to inspect will result in the cashier being held financially accountable for the counterfeit note.

## 9. INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY, POS SECURITY & COMMUNICATIONS

### 9.1. Password Confidentiality & POS Credentials
- Every cashier is issued a unique POS login ID, PIN, or password.
- **Never share passwords:** Employees must never disclose their PIN or allow another employee to process sales under their user profile.
- All actions, sales, voids, and cash movements executed under an employee's user ID are deemed to have been executed by that employee.

### 9.2. Personal Mobile Phone Policy
- Personal mobile phones, smartphones, and entertainment devices must be kept in personal lockers or bags during working hours.
- Making or receiving personal calls, browsing social media (TikTok, Facebook, Instagram, YouTube), gaming, or texting behind the sales counter is strictly prohibited while on duty.
- Urgent emergency family calls may be received briefly off the sales floor with supervisor notice.

### 9.3. Company Data Protection & Social Media
- Employees shall not photograph, screenshot, or distribute company documents, financial records, pricing lists, sales figures, or POS screens on social media or messaging platforms.
- Employees are strictly prohibited from posting disparaging, defamatory, or damaging comments about Inventive Harvest, its management, colleagues, or customers on social media.

## 10. HEALTH, SAFETY, HYGIENE & ENVIRONMENT (HSE)

### 10.1. General Workplace Cleanliness & Hygiene
- Every branch must be kept spotless, hygienic, and organized.
- Daily cleaning tasks (sweeping, mopping, wiping glass counters, dusting stock, emptying trash cans) must be performed faithfully.
- Aisles and walkways must remain clear of cardboard boxes, clutter, and tripping hazards at all times.

### 10.2. Fire Safety & Emergency Preparedness
- Fire extinguishers must be kept unobstructed and inspected regularly.
- All employees must familiarize themselves with the location of fire extinguishers, main electrical circuit breakers, and emergency exits.
- In case of fire or armed robbery, employee personal safety is paramount: preserve human life first, follow emergency procedures, and contact emergency services and management immediately.

### 10.3. Drug, Alcohol & Tobacco Free Workplace
- Inventive Harvest maintains an absolute **zero-tolerance policy** for alcohol and illicit narcotics.
- Employees are strictly prohibited from reporting to work under the influence of alcohol or drugs, consuming alcohol or narcotics during work hours or lunch breaks, or bringing intoxicating substances onto Company premises.
- Smoking or vaping inside shops, storerooms, or near customer entrances is strictly prohibited.

## 11. DISCIPLINARY FRAMEWORK, CODE OF OFFENSES & GRIEVANCE MECHANISM

### 11.1. Disciplinary Philosophy
The Company’s disciplinary system is designed to be corrective, fair, transparent, and consistent. Disciplinary action will be administered in compliance with natural justice and relevant national employment codes.

### 11.2. Progressive Disciplinary Sanctions
1. **Verbal Warning:** Issued for minor first-time infractions; recorded on the employee’s HR file (valid for 3 months).
2. **First Written Warning:** Issued for repeated minor infractions or serious first offenses (valid for 6 months).
3. **Final Written Warning:** Issued for serious misconduct or continued infractions following a written warning (valid for 12 months).
4. **Summary Dismissal:** Termination of employment without notice or pay in lieu, applicable in cases of Gross Misconduct after a formal hearing.

### 11.3. Schedule of Disciplinary Offenses & Penalties

| Offense Category | Nature of Infraction | 1st Offense | 2nd Offense | 3rd Offense |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Minor Offenses** | Late arrival (<15 mins) without notice | Verbal Warning | Written Warning | Final Written Warning |
| | Unkempt uniform / failure to wear name badge | Verbal Warning | Written Warning | Final Written Warning |
| | Eating behind the sales counter | Verbal Warning | Written Warning | Final Written Warning |
| | Personal phone use at active sales desk | Written Warning | Final Written Warning | Disciplinary Hearing |
| **Serious Offenses** | Failure to balance cash till / unverified shortage | Written Warning | Final Written Warning | Disciplinary Hearing |
| | Failure to issue official POS receipt to customer | Final Written Warning | Disciplinary Hearing | Dismissal |
| | Unauthorized shift absence (1–2 days) | Written Warning | Final Written Warning | Disciplinary Hearing |
| | Insubordination / refusal of reasonable order | Final Written Warning | Disciplinary Hearing | Dismissal |
| | Careless handling resulting in damaged stock | Written Warning | Final Written Warning | Disciplinary Hearing |
| **Gross Misconduct** | Theft, fraud, embezzlement, or cash misappropriation | **Dismissal** | — | — |
| | Unrecorded sales / selling off-book / pocketing cash | **Dismissal** | — | — |
| | Falsification of POS records, receipts, or attendance | **Dismissal** | — | — |
| | Borrowing cash from till / placing IOUs in drawer | **Dismissal** | — | — |
| | Reporting to work intoxicated or using drugs on duty | **Dismissal** | — | — |
| | Physical violence, assault, or threatening behaviour | **Dismissal** | — | — |
| | Sexual harassment or severe verbal abuse | **Dismissal** | — | — |
| | Unauthorized disclosure of confidential business data | **Dismissal** | — | — |
| | Job desertion (absence for 3+ consecutive days) | **Dismissal** | — | — |

### 11.4. Disciplinary Hearing Procedures
Before the imposition of dismissal, demotion, or suspension without pay:
1. **Notice of Charge:** The Employee shall receive written notice stating the specific charges, facts, and the scheduled hearing date at least **48 hours in advance**.
2. **Right to Representation:** The Employee has the right to be accompanied and represented by a fellow work colleague or shop steward.
3. **Right to Defense:** The Employee shall have full opportunity to state their defense, call relevant witnesses, and cross-examine evidence presented.
4. **Impartial Determination:** The hearing committee shall evaluate the evidence objectively and provide a reasoned, written verdict and sanction.

### 11.5. Grievance Procedure
Employees who feel aggrieved, unfairly treated, or have complaints regarding working conditions must follow this structured grievance process:
- **Step 1 (Immediate Supervisor):** Verbally present the grievance to the Branch Supervisor within three (3) days of occurrence. The supervisor must address and resolve it within forty-eight (48) hours.
- **Step 2 (Formal Grievance to Store Manager / HR):** If unresolved at Step 1, the employee may submit a written **Employee Grievance Form** to the Store Manager or Managing Director. A formal meeting will be held within five (5) working days to resolve the matter.
- **Step 3 (Executive Review):** If still unsatisfied, the matter may be escalated to the Executive / Managing Director whose determination on internal operational matters shall be final.
- *Protection Against Retaliation:* No employee shall suffer victimisation, discrimination, or disadvantage for raising a genuine grievance in good faith.
