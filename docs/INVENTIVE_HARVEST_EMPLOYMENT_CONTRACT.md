### SECTION 6: STRICT CASH HANDLING, POS & FINANCIAL CONTROLS

6.1. **Strict Point-of-Sale (POS) Protocol:**  
- **Every single customer sale must be processed through the official Point-of-Sale (POS) terminal** (or entered into the pre-numbered, official manual sales backup ledger immediately in the event of documented power or network downtime) *before* goods are bagged, handed over, or removed from the premises.
- Under no circumstances may goods be released on credit, deferred billing, or informal "trust" arrangements without the prior written approval of the Managing Director.
- Under-ringing items, manipulating barcode scans, applying unauthorized discounts, or issuing manual receipts without authorization is strictly prohibited and constitutes an act of fraud.

6.2. **Cash Drawer & Float Integrity:**  
- The Employee is responsible for the exact verification of the opening float at the start of each shift. Both incoming and outgoing staff must count, verify, and sign the official **Shift Handover / Cash Register Control Form**.
- Personal cash, handbags, personal wallets, or foreign money must **never** be placed inside the company till drawer or cash box.
- All customer payments (Cash, Mobile Money / EcoCash / Airtel Money, Debit/Credit Card, Bank Transfer) must be recorded under the exact payment method in the POS system.

6.3. **Restricted Financial Buckets (Operations, Savings, Black Box, Rent Reserve):**  
- Inventive Harvest maintains strict separation between shop sales revenue, Petty Cash floats, Operations funds, dedicated Savings, the Black Box emergency reserve, and the Rent Reserve.
- The Employee shall not divert, swap, borrow, cross-allocate, or commingle funds between these designated buckets unless accompanied by an authorized executive voucher signed by the Managing Director.
- Taking money from the till for personal emergencies, casual advances, IOUs ("chits"), or unapproved purchases is treated as criminal misappropriation.

6.4. **Cash Variances & Discrepancies:**  
- At the conclusion of each trading shift, an end-of-day Z-report / POS sales closing summary must be generated and physically matched against the cash in the drawer.
- Any discrepancy (shortage or surplus) must be documented on the daily closing sheet and reported immediately to the Branch Supervisor or Managing Director.
- Cash overages belong to the Company and must be booked in full; taking or pocketing surplus cash is theft.
- Unexplained shortages arising from gross negligence, failure to count change, or violation of cash handling procedures shall be thoroughly investigated and may result in disciplinary action and payroll recovery.

### SECTION 7: STOCK CONTROL, MERCHANDISE & ASSET PROTECTION

7.1. **Receiving Stock:** All inventory received at the branch from suppliers or from the central warehouse must be meticulously inspected, counted, checked against the delivery note / invoice, and formally booked into the POS inventory system before being placed on shop shelves.

7.2. **Stock Transfers Between Branches:**  
- No merchandise may be moved or transferred between Dub Dub, Kipasa, Trade Center, or any warehouse without an authorized, pre-numbered **Stock Transfer Voucher (STV) / Stock Issue Voucher (SIV)** signed by both the issuing and receiving staff.
- Discrepancies during transit must be flagged immediately upon receipt.

7.3. **Stocktaking & Audits:**  
- The Employee shall actively participate in routine, monthly, and surprise stock counts.
- Any damaged, expired, or unsaleable merchandise must be segregated and recorded in the Damaged Goods Register for formal management write-off. No staff member may unilaterally discard or consume damaged items.

7.4. **Search and Security Rights:**  
To safeguard company assets against internal shrinkage, the Employee acknowledges and agrees that the Employer’s designated security personnel or management representatives have the lawful right to conduct reasonable searches of the Employee’s bags, parcels, lockers, and outer garments upon entering or departing Company premises.

- The Employee shall not establish, operate, advise, or have a direct financial interest in any retail business competing directly with Inventive Harvest.

### SECTION 11: DISCIPLINE, MISCONDUCT & GRIEVANCES

11.1. **Disciplinary Framework:**  
Disciplinary matters shall be administered fairly, transparently, and in alignment with natural justice, the registered National Employment Code / relevant collective bargaining framework, and the **Inventive Harvest Workplace Policies**.

11.2. **Disciplinary Actions:** Misconduct may attract the following progressive sanctions depending on gravity:
1. Verbal Warning (documented on staff file; valid for 3 months);
2. First Written Warning (valid for 6 months);
3. Final Written Warning (valid for 12 months);
4. Suspension with or without pay pending formal investigation;
5. Summary Dismissal following a formal disciplinary hearing.

11.3. **Acts of Gross Misconduct (Summary Dismissal):**  
The following offenses constitute gross misconduct entitling the Company to terminate employment summarily without notice or terminal pay in lieu of notice, following a fair hearing:
- Theft, embezzlement, unauthorized possession, or misappropriation of Company cash, stock, equipment, or assets;
- Falsification, forgery, or fraudulent manipulation of POS records, receipts, cash counts, invoices, or attendance logs;
- Unrecorded sales, off-book trading, unauthorized discounting, or diverting cash takings;
- Gross insubordination or wilful refusal to execute lawful, reasonable managerial instructions;
- Reporting to duty under the influence of alcohol, narcotics, or intoxicating substances;
- Violent conduct, physical fighting, threatening behavior, sexual harassment, or verbal abuse on Company premises;
- Wilful destruction or gross negligent damage to Company property, premises, or computerized systems;
- Breach of confidentiality or unauthorized disclosure of proprietary trade data;
- Unauthorized absence from work exceeding three (3) consecutive days (desertion).

11.4. **Disciplinary Procedure:** Prior to any disciplinary sanction involving dismissal, the Employee shall be issued with a written charge sheet stating the allegations, given at least **forty-eight (48) hours** to prepare a defense, afforded the right to be heard before an impartial disciplinary committee, and permitted to be represented by a fellow employee or shop steward.

11.5. **Grievance Resolution:** Any work-related complaint, dispute, or unfair treatment concern must be raised through the internal grievance mechanism described in Section 10 of the Workplace Policies, starting with the immediate supervisor and escalating to the Managing Director.
